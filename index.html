The working route

From request to educator decision.

Each pilot has a defined intake, production stage, review handoff and revision boundary. Nothing is silently treated as final.

01

Scope

Choose marking or exam production and define the subject, volume, rules and deadline.

02

Intake

Provide the approved files: learner work and rubric, or grade, term, scope and assessment requirements.

03

Prepare

HOMS creates the structured draft outputs and the supporting review trail.

04

Approve

The educator or subject expert reviews, changes and approves before any classroom use.

Professional boundary: HOMS supports assessment work. It does not replace educator judgement, institutional moderation, or subject-expert approval.
