The HOMS workflow

A faster first pass.
A stronger final judgement.

HOMS handles the repeatable labour around assessment while keeping interpretation, moderation and final responsibility firmly with the educator.

01

Bring in the work

Upload individual or batch submissions, import a rubric, or connect an existing learning workflow.

02

Generate a first pass

HOMS analyses every response against your criteria and prepares scores, comments and in-text annotations.

03

Review with authority

The lecturer remains the decision-maker. Inspect, adjust, approve and add the human judgement only you can provide.

04

Return richer feedback

Export annotated work, release results, or return feedback through your institutional workflow.

Built around real marking work

Not a chatbot with a red pen.

HOMS is an assessment workspace designed around criteria, evidence, feedback and review.

Rubric-native marking

Assessment starts with your criteria, levels and weightings, keeping feedback aligned to the task you designed.

In-text annotations

Praise, questions, corrections and suggestions are attached to the student’s own words, where they are most useful.

Individual and group work

Handle single submissions, class batches and group assignments without rebuilding the workflow each time.

Cohort-level insight

See score distributions, recurring misconceptions and common strengths before the marks disappear into a spreadsheet.

Human-governed AI

HOMS supports professional judgement rather than impersonating it. Nothing becomes final without lecturer oversight.

LMS-ready handoff

Prepare downloadable results and structured feedback for return to students or onward integration.

Human authority by design

AI proposes.
Educators decide.

Every mark carries professional consequences. HOMS is built to assist judgement, expose its basis and keep approval in human hands.
